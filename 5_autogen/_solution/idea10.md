What a delightful and thoughtful idea for the Miso Maple Glazed Cauliflower Tacos! Your emphasis on flavor, texture, and sustainability is commendable, and it presents a wonderful opportunity for an interactive dining experience. Here are some additional thoughts and enhancements that can further elevate your concept:

### Flavor Enhancements:
1. **Spice It Up**: Consider incorporating a touch of heat to the miso maple glaze by adding a bit of sriracha or chili garlic sauce. This will create a delightful contrast with the sweetness of the maple syrup.
  
2. **Fresh Herbs**: Alongside cilantro, adding fresh mint or basil to the slaw can introduce a refreshing element that complements the miso flavors beautifully.

3. **Citrus Zest**: For an extra burst of flavor, zest a lime or orange into the avocado-cilantro crema. It will add brightness and elevate the overall taste profile.

### Texture Variations:
1. **Roasted Nuts or Seeds**: Introduce some toasted sesame seeds, pumpkin seeds, or even crushed walnuts as a topping. This will add a satisfying crunch and additional nutritional benefits.

2. **Grilled Corn**: Adding charred corn kernels to the slaw or as a topping will not only enhance the texture but also bring a smoky sweetness that pairs well with the tacos.

### Interactive Experience Ideas:
1. **DIY Taco Bar**: Instead of just assembling their tacos, guests can be encouraged to create their own unique combinations. Provide a variety of toppings, such as pickled jalapeños, assorted hot sauces, or even a mango salsa, allowing for a personalized experience.

2. **Cooking Stations**: If you have the space and resources, consider setting up cooking stations where guests can help with light preparation, such as making the crema or assembling the slaw. This not only fosters participation but can also become a fun social activity.

3. **Flavor Pairing Card**: Create a small card for each guest that suggests different flavor pairings and combinations they might try. This encourages creativity and exploration in their taco assembly.

### Sustainability Focus:
1. **Local Ingredient Spotlight**: Feature a “local ingredient of the month” that can be swapped into the recipe seasonally. This keeps the dish fresh and offers an educational aspect about local produce.

2. **Composting Demonstration**: If you’re serving this meal at an event, consider a small demonstration on how to compost vegetable scraps or even provide mini compost bins for guests to take home, tying back to sustainability.

### Presentation Tips:
1. **Colorful Garnishes**: Consider garnishing the tacos with edible flowers or microgreens for added visual appeal, making the dish even more inviting.

2. **Themed Table Setting**: Use vibrant, eco-friendly tableware and colorful linens to enhance the dining experience. A well-set table can elevate the entire meal.

Your refined dish concept is both innovative and inviting, making it a perfect fit for a range of occasions. By incorporating these enhancements, you can create an even more engaging and memorable culinary experience that resonates with your guests. Keep shining your culinary light!
