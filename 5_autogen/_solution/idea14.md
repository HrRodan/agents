How about a **Savory Turmeric & Coconut Quinoa Bowl with Miso-Maple Glazed Vegetables**? This dish beautifully marries global flavors while being packed with nutrition!

### Recipe: Savory Turmeric & Coconut Quinoa Bowl

#### Ingredients:

**For the Quinoa:**
- 1 cup quinoa, rinsed
- 1 can (13.5 oz) coconut milk
- 1 cup vegetable broth
- 1 tsp turmeric powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- Zest of 1 lime

**For the Glazed Vegetables:**
- 1 cup diced sweet potatoes
- 1 cup broccoli florets
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 2 tbsp miso paste (white or yellow)
- 2 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp grated ginger

**For Garnish:**
- Fresh cilantro, chopped
- Sliced avocado
- Toasted sesame seeds
- Lime wedges

#### Instructions:

1. **Cook the Quinoa:**
   - In a pot, combine the rinsed quinoa, coconut milk, vegetable broth, turmeric, salt, and lime zest. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer, cover, and cook for 15 minutes or until the quinoa is fluffy and the liquid is absorbed. Fluff with a fork.

2. **Prepare the Glazed Vegetables:**
   - Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). In a bowl, whisk together miso paste, maple syrup, sesame oil, soy sauce, and grated ginger. Add the sweet potatoes, broccoli, and red bell pepper, tossing to coat evenly.
   - Spread the vegetables on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper. Roast for 20-25 minutes, stirring halfway, until tender and caramelized.

3. **Assemble the Bowl:**
   - In a serving bowl, layer the turmeric coconut quinoa as the base. Top with the miso-maple glazed vegetables, and add slices of avocado. Sprinkle with toasted sesame seeds and fresh cilantro. Serve with lime wedges on the side for an extra zing.

### Why This Dish Works:
- **Flavor Fusion:** The combination of coconut and turmeric in the quinoa provides a rich, creamy base, while the miso-maple glaze adds umami and sweetness to the roasted vegetables.
- **Nutritional Benefits:** Quinoa is a complete protein and is high in fiber. Turmeric is known for its anti-inflammatory properties, and the vegetables add a plethora of vitamins and antioxidants.
- **Visual Appeal:** The vibrant colors from the roasted veggies and garnishes create a visually enticing dish that’s sure to impress!

This recipe not only champions global ingredients but also transforms traditional elements into a modern, health-conscious dish. Enjoy your culinary adventure! 🌿✨
