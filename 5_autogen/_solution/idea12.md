### Ingredients:
#### For the Tacos:
- 2 large sweet potatoes, peeled and diced
- 1 can of black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- Olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Corn tortillas (or your choice of tortilla)

#### For the Spicy Pineapple Salsa:
- 1 cup fresh pineapple, diced
- 1 jalapeño, finely chopped (remove seeds for less heat)
- 1/4 red onion, finely chopped
- Juice of 1 lime
- Fresh cilantro, chopped
- Salt, to taste

#### Optional Toppings:
- Avocado slices
- Crumbled feta or cotija cheese
- Fresh lime wedges

### Instructions:

1. **Roast the Sweet Potatoes**: Preheat your oven to 425°F (220°C). Toss the diced sweet potatoes with olive oil, smoked paprika, cumin, salt, and pepper. Spread them out on a baking sheet and roast for about 25-30 minutes, or until they’re golden and tender.

2. **Prepare the Salsa**: While the sweet potatoes are roasting, mix together the diced pineapple, jalapeño, red onion, lime juice, cilantro, and a pinch of salt in a bowl. Let the salsa sit for a bit to allow the flavors to meld. The sweet, spicy, and tangy notes will dance together beautifully!

3. **Warm the Tortillas**: In a dry skillet, warm your tortillas on medium heat for about 30 seconds on each side until they're soft and pliable.

4. **Assemble the Tacos**: Layer the roasted sweet potatoes and black beans onto each tortilla. Top with a generous spoonful of the spicy pineapple salsa, and add any optional toppings you desire like creamy avocado or crumbled cheese.

5. **Serve and Enjoy**: Squeeze fresh lime juice over the top for that zesty finish. Gather your friends or family and enjoy these delightful tacos bursting with flavor!

### Why This Works:
The sweetness of the roasted sweet potatoes pairs beautifully with the savory black beans, while the pineapple salsa adds a refreshing kick that will tantalize your taste buds. It’s a perfect balance of flavors and textures that feels both comforting and exciting!
